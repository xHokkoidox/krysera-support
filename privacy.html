Krysera Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Krysera handles information when you use our mobile game. We respect your privacy and aim to be transparent about data practices.

Information Collection

Krysera does not require account registration and does not directly request your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or payment information.

Our third-party advertising partner, Google AdMob, may collect certain information through its SDK, including:

  • Device identifiers
  • Advertising data and information about ad interactions
  • App interaction information
  • Approximate location information, where permitted
  • Crash reports and performance diagnostics

The information collected depends on SDK configuration, device settings, consent choices, and applicable laws.

Advertising

Krysera uses Google AdMob to display third-party advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may use information to deliver, measure, and improve advertisements, subject to applicable privacy settings and consent requirements.

Data Sharing

Information collected by third-party advertising and service providers may be processed by those providers in accordance with their own privacy policies. We do not sell personal information directly.

Data Retention

Data collected by third-party services is retained according to the applicable provider's retention policies.

Children's Privacy

Krysera is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe personal information has been collected inappropriately, please contact us.

Your Privacy Choices

You can manage available advertising and privacy settings through your device settings and any consent options presented in the app. You may also contact us with privacy-related questions.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.
